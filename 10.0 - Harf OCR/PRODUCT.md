# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Staff in a government office who receive official Arabic letters and reports as phone photos, often forwarded on WhatsApp, with stamps, handwritten notes and highlighter on them. They turn those photos into clean Word or PDF documents on the office letterhead. Most review work happens at a desk PC; some staff check or fix a draft on a phone. Some users are older, and most are not technical.

## Product Purpose

Photos go in, in the right order. The text is read by a free OCR engine or an AI engine. The user checks the text against the photo, fixes it, and produces the final file with the chosen header template. Anything unclear is never blocked: it goes into the output highlighted, with a comment saying why. Success means a reviewer can trust the document without reopening the photos.

## Brand Personality

Calm, official, careful. It should feel like a well-kept desk in a records office: paper, ink, a stamp pad. The letter is the hero and the interface stays quiet around it. The voice is plain Arabic, short and polite, never chatty.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards: gradient buttons, glass cards, big metric tiles.
- Consumer "scanner" apps with loud colors, mascots and upsell banners.
- Old government portals: cramped tables, tiny gray text, ten fonts on one page.

## Design Principles

1. The document comes first. The photo and the text get the space; tools sit at the edges.
2. Flags are the workflow. Highlighted, commented spots are how review gets done, so they must be easy to see, reach and resolve.
3. Never lose work. Save state is always visible, and destructive actions ask once, clearly.
4. One obvious next step per screen: add photos, read, review, produce.
5. Familiar over clever. Standard controls, predictable places, Arabic RTL done properly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA contrast at minimum, tested in light and dark.
- Text that stays readable for older staff when zoomed (the layout holds at 125% and above), with touch targets of at least 44px on phones.
- Full keyboard paths with visible focus on every control, including the editor, page list, column resizers and dialogs.
- Respect reduced-motion settings.
